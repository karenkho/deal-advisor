# Bid Bulletin No. 01: Human Resource Information System (HRIS)

**Synthetic document for a proof of concept. The company, people, figures and contact details are fictional.**

Issued by: Kalinaw Trading Corporation (fictional), Human Resources and Administration Department

Date issued: 5 December 2026

## 1. Purpose

This bulletin amends the Terms of Reference for the Human Resource Information System. Where it differs from the Terms of Reference, this bulletin governs. Prospective vendors raised questions about the number of leave approval levels and about the delivery period, and the Company has reviewed both with its management committee.

## 2. Leave approval levels

Sections 5.3 and 12.2 of the Terms of Reference stated the approval levels differently. The Company has confirmed the following.

### 2.1 Approval levels

Under Section 5.3, leave applications shall be approved in three levels: first by the immediate supervisor, then by the department head, and finally by the HR manager.

## 3. Delivery period

The Company recognises that the original period leaves little room for user acceptance testing and live operation.

### 3.1 Revised delivery period

Under Section 14.2, the System shall be delivered, accepted and in live operation within six (6) months from the issuance of the Notice to Proceed.

## 4. Additional requirement

Employees asked to keep a personal copy of their leave history for their own records.

### 4.1 Leave ledger download

Under Section 5.4, employees shall be able to download their own leave ledger for the current year as a PDF file.

## 5. Other provisions

All other provisions of the Terms of Reference remain in effect. Vendors should reflect this bulletin in their proposals and acknowledge its receipt in the proposal cover letter.

Clarifications on this bulletin may be submitted in writing to the designated contact person within three (3) working days from its date of issue. The Company does not expect to change the submission deadline as a result of this bulletin.

Issued for and on behalf of the Company by the HR and Administration Department.
