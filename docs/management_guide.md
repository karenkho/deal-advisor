# Deal Advisor: what to try

Log in with the password you were given, then enter your name and pick a role. The role is self-declared for this POC and logged, and you can switch it in the sidebar, so one person can walk through the whole flow. All sample data is fictional.

## The flow

Create a sample with **Create sample HRIS opportunity** (as a BDM), then follow the seven steps. The banner always says where things stand and whose turn it is.

1. **Intake (BDM).** Client and commercial details, and documents. Bid bulletins can be added at any time; after approval they open a change request.
2. **BRD (Solutions Specialist).** Review each requirement, edit in the table, or download the Word file, edit it and upload it again to see a side-by-side diff first. Approval makes v1.0. Requirement numbers never change, and **Versions** shows exactly what changed between any two versions.
3. **Estimate & Timeline (Solutions Specialist).** AI-generated estimates and timeline for B AI-Augmented and C AI-First, from the approved BRD. Adjust sizes, activity hours, team and assumptions; the calculators recompute at once. Validate the rows and the checklist, then approve. After a BRD change, only what changed needs validating again.
4. **Proposal (BDM, then BDM and Legal/Management).** The BDM picks the scenario and generates an internal review copy and a client copy. Both a BDM and a Legal/Management reviewer must approve. An edited file that changes the price or scope is refused: commercial changes go through the estimate.
5. **Award (BDM).** Awarded unlocks contracts. Not awarded or Withdrawn closes the opportunity, read-only.
6. **Contracts (BDM).** The SOW comes from the approved versions. The MSA is the company template, filled by code; its legal wording is untouched.
7. **Final Approval.** BDM (commercial details), Solutions Specialist (scope), Legal/Management (MSA, with its clause checklist). With all three and a passing consistency check, the opportunity is Contract-Ready.

## Good to know

- Everything is saved as it happens. Close the browser and pick it up later, from any session.
- AI outputs are drafts. People approve everything, and every step is in the **Audit trail**.
- Internal copies and estimate summaries hold pricing: they are never placed in client folders.
- Only an Admin sees rates. Uploaded rates stay in that browser session only.
